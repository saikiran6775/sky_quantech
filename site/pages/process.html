Good Solutions Start With Good Questions

Before choosing a technology approach, we focus on understanding what needs to be solved, who the solution is for, and how it will be used.

01

WHAT

What problem needs to be solved? Exploring core constraints, operational friction points, latency bottlenecks, and measurable success criteria.

Key Focus Constraint mapping, business impact, failure modes
02
Personas & Operations

WHO

Who will use or benefit from the solution? Identifying operators, analytical stakeholders, system maintainers, and downstream enterprise end-users.

Key Focus Stakeholder workflows, permissions, cognitive load

HOW

What technology approach makes sense? Evaluating practical tools, suitable approaches and the effort involved.
