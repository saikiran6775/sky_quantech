## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-precision corporate technology aesthetic tailored for enterprise computational intelligence and automated decision systems. The visual tone is authoritative, highly disciplined, and rigorously uncluttered. It projects calculated confidence through deep optical contrast, structured geometric hierarchy, and crisp modern typography.

The design movement merges **Corporate Modernism** with **Engineered Minimalism**:
- **Structured Precision**: Interfaces rely on disciplined alignment, restrained surface boundaries, and explicit information density rather than ornamental decoration.
- **Controlled Luminescence**: Vivid electric and highlight blues are reserved strictly for interactive vectors, active system states, and visual focal points against deep navy or sterile light surfaces.
- **Enterprise Utility**: High-efficiency readability, zero-latency legibility across complex data matrices, and stark visual clarity take precedence across all viewport configurations.

## Colors

The palette employs a multi-tiered corporate blue spectrum balanced against a clinical ice-blue ground and pure white container surfaces.

### Functional Roles & Semantic Mapping
- **Primary (`#0020A0`)**: Structural enterprise blue. Used for dominant calls-to-action, active component fills, header rules, and primary visual anchors.
- **Secondary / Electric Blue (`#0050F0`)**: High-visibility interactive hue. Applied to key interactive triggers, secondary status indicators, dynamic graphs, and hover transitions.
- **Tertiary / Highlight Blue (`#0078FF`)**: Precision illumination token. Utilized for focus rings, metric callouts, progress tracks, active tab indicators, and subtle informational pills.
- **Neutral / Deep Blue (`#001060`)**: The core anchor. Forms the primary typographic token, dominant navigational framing, dark borders, and maximum-contrast structural elements.
- **Dark Corporate Blue (`#001070`)**: Structural secondary neutral. Used for sub-navigation backgrounds, elevated dark cards, and deep contrast panels.
- **Background Base (`#F5F8FF`)**: Light blue canvas wash. Replaces generic gray to maintain continuous brand temperature behind cards and containers.
- **Surface Card (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white. Reserved for elevated card containers, input fields, modal surfaces, and canvas segmentation.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy couples **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural display and section headings with **Inter** for technical content, body text, numerical telemetry, and interface labels.

### Rules & Application
- **Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Rendered in semi-bold (600) and bold (700) weights with tight negative tracking to ensure confident enterprise authority.
- **Body & Data (Inter)**: Set at regular (400) and medium (500) weights to provide sustained readability across data grids, logs, and form matrices.
- **Labels & Overlines**: Rendered in uppercase with increased letter tracking (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) for technical telemetry, status badges, and table column heads.
- **Contrast Discipline**: Main headings use Deep Blue (`#001060`). Secondary descriptive copy defaults to Deep Blue rendered at 70% opacity, maintaining visual hierarchy without introducing disconnected neutral grays.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column responsive fluid grid operating on a foundational 8px baseline rhythm (with a 4px sub-grid for micro-adjustments within controls and badges).

### Grid Structure & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) margins, 16px (`1rem`) gutters. Content stacks vertically; interactive matrices convert to full-width linear flows.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8 columns, 32px (`2rem`) margins, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters. Side rails collapse into compact icon rails or slide-over sheets.
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px)**: 12 columns, 48px (`3rem`) margins, 32px (`2rem`) gutters. Main workspace caps at a maximum bounded width of 1440px, centered with automatic margins on wide viewports.

### Spacing Rules
- **Internal Density**: Components maintain a tight 8px–12px padding footprint to support enterprise data concentration.
- **Card Padding**: Standard desktop containers use `space-lg` (24px) internal padding; high-density analytical dashboards drop to `space-md` (16px).
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Related section stacks employ `space-xl` (32px), while top-level structural layout modules separate via `space-3xl` (64px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined combination of **low-contrast cool-toned outlines** and **ambient blue-tinted shadows**. Heavy skeuomorphic drop shadows and blur gimmicks are avoided in favor of crisp, engineered depth planes.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#F5F8FF` base background. Flat, zero elevation.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Cards & Panels)**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px structural border (`rgba(0, 16, 96, 0.08)`). Elevation shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(0, 16, 96, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(0, 16, 96, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Hover & Flyouts)**: Elevated white surface. Elevation shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(0, 16, 96, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -2px rgba(0, 32, 160, 0.06)`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals, Overlays & Popovers)**: Maximum surface elevation. Elevation shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(0, 16, 96, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(0, 32, 160, 0.08)`. Border shifts to `rgba(0, 16, 96, 0.12)`.

### Border Discipline
All functional boundaries leverage cool tinted strokes derived directly from Deep Blue (`#001060`) rather than standard gray hex codes. Focused elements introduce an active 2px outer ring in Highlight Blue (`#0078FF`) with a 2px white offset gap.

## Shapes

The design system adheres to a **Soft** shape language (`roundedness: 1`), conveying corporate discipline and architectural rigor. Rounding is applied systematically to prevent playful distraction while softening sharp technical vectors.

### Corner Radius Standards
- **Base (4px / 0.25rem)**: Standard buttons, input fields, checkboxes, dropdown items, tooltips, and code blocks.
- **Large (8px / 0.5rem)**: Cards, modular panels, data table containers, and modal dialogs.
- **Extra Large (12px / 0.75rem)**: Outer application frames and segmented grouping wrappers.
- **Pill Exception (9999px)**: Reserved exclusively for status indicators, micro tags, and numeric badge counters to separate state metadata from interactive rectangular triggers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#0020A0`, text `#FFFFFF`, border none, 4px corner radius. Padding: `10px 20px` (body-md semi-bold). Hover: Background `#0050F0`. Active: Background `#001070`. Focus ring: 2px solid `#0078FF` with 2px offset.
- **Secondary**: Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0020A0`, 1px solid `rgba(0, 32, 160, 0.2)`. Hover: Background `#F5F8FF`, border `#0050F0`, text `#0050F0`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Background transparent, text `#0020A0`. Hover: Background `rgba(0, 80, 240, 0.06)`, text `#0050F0`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs**: Height 40px. Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid `rgba(0, 16, 96, 0.16)`, 4px corner radius. Text `#001060`, placeholder `rgba(0, 16, 96, 0.4)`. Focus: 1px solid `#0050F0`, box-shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 120, 255, 0.18)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 16x16px base, 3px corner radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios. Unchecked: 1.5px solid `rgba(0, 16, 96, 0.24)`, white background. Checked: `#0020A0` fill, pure white check/dot icon. Focus: 2px ring `#0078FF`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Informational Chips**: Height 24px, 9999px pill radius. Background `rgba(0, 80, 240, 0.08)`, text `#0050F0`, label-sm font weight.
- **Active State Badges**: 6px pulsing or solid indicator dot in `#0078FF` or `#0050F0` paired with uppercase label-sm typography.
- **Filter Chips**: Height 32px, 4px radius. Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `rgba(0, 16, 96, 0.12)`, text `#001060`. Selected: Background `#0020A0`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `#0020A0`.

### Cards & Modular Containers
- **Standard Card**: White `#FFFFFF` ground, 8px radius, 1px solid `rgba(0, 16, 96, 0.08)`, 24px internal padding, Tier 1 ambient shadow.
- **Interactive Action Card**: Standard card treatment with hover state transitioning border to `rgba(0, 80, 240, 0.4)` and elevation to Tier 2.

### Lists & Data Tables
- **Table Headers**: Background `rgba(0, 16, 96, 0.02)`, height 44px, bottom border 1px solid `rgba(0, 16, 96, 0.1)`. Label-md uppercase text in `#001060` at 70% opacity.
- **Table Rows**: Height 48px, background `#FFFFFF`, bottom border 1px solid `rgba(0, 16, 96, 0.05)`. Hover: Background `rgba(0, 80, 240, 0.02)`.
- **List Items**: Clean horizontal flex containers, 12px vertical padding, separated by `1px solid rgba(0, 16, 96, 0.06)`.

### Technical UI Extensions
- **KPI Metric Block**: Surface card hosting an overline label (`label-sm`), an oversized metric value (`headline-lg` in `#001060`), and a directional badge in `#0050F0`.
- **Status Signal Strip**: A 3px left-side border accent in `#0050F0` or `#0078FF` applied to standard cards to highlight prioritized computation streams or critical operational records.
